I am the bass player in my band, and I almost exclusively use the 5-string bass. 
The added low B string is awesome because it vibrates at around 31 Hz, and considering the fact that most human can only hear frequency as low as 20Hz, it is really pushing our hearing limits. You will literally feel the grove in your chest before your ear processes the note. I have one that is made by schecter, and I absolutely love it.
